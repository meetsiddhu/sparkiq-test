Bring your own AI (optional)

SparkIQ works fully on its own, offline, for free. If you’d like richer open-ended conversations, a parent can connect an AI in the app’s parent area — your choice, your control.

  • Use the SparkIQ server, or paste your own provider key (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI)
  • Your details stay on your device — never in anything a child can reach
  • Every AI reply passes a child-safety filter, with usage caps
  • Turn it off any time — the app keeps working without it

🔒 Safety and privacy are the whole point

SparkIQ was designed around child protection first, features second.

We barely know your child

A child profile is a nickname, a year, and a curriculum. No real name, no photo, no location, no birthdate. A parent email is the only real identifier — and only if you choose cloud backup.

Nothing is sold or tracked

No advertising, no third-party trackers, no marketing profiles, no data sale. Analytics, where they exist, are aggregate only.

Care is built in

Payments and settings sit behind a parent PIN. If a child ever expresses distress, the app points them to a trusted adult and Kids Helpline (1800 55 1800).
